{Q}各语言求二进制集合大小（置1位数）方法？{A}Python：`s.bit_count()`；Java：`Integer.bitCount(s)`；C++：`__builtin_popcount(s)`；Go：`bits.OnesCount(s)`{E}

{Q}各语言求二进制长度方法？{A}Python：`s.bit_length()`；Java：`32-Integer.numberOfLeadingZeros(s)`；C++：`__lg(s)+1`；Go：`bits.Len(s)`{E}

{Q}各语言求二进制集合最大元素方法？{A}Python：`s.bit_length()-1`；Java：`31-Integer.numberOfLeadingZeros(s)`；C++：`__lg(s)`；Go：`bits.Len(s)-1`{E}

{Q}各语言求二进制集合最小元素方法？{A}Python：`(s&-s).bit_length()-1`；Java：`Integer.numberOfTrailingZeros(s)`；C++：`__builtin_ctz(s)`；Go：`bits.TrailingZeros(s)`{E}
